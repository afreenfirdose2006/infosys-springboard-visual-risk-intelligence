Privacy Policy

Visual Risk and AI Intelligence

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Visual Risk and AI Intelligence. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide application features, we may collect and process information entered by users, including:

  • Username and registration details.
  • Study information, academic performance, and progress.
  • Financial information entered into the Financial Management module.
  • Fitness-related information entered into the Fitness Tracking module.
  • Questions and prompts submitted through the AI Assistant.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided by users may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Displaying personalized dashboards.
  • Tracking study and fitness progress.
  • Providing financial insights and visual summaries.
  • Generating AI-based responses and suggestions.
  • Improving application functionality and user experience.

3. AI Assistant and Third-Party Services

Our AI Assistant uses Google's Gemini API to process prompts and generate responses.

  • Prompts submitted to the AI Assistant may be sent to Google's Gemini API.
  • Processing of such information is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.
  • AI responses may not always be accurate or complete.
  • Users should avoid sharing passwords, banking credentials, or other highly sensitive information.

4. Data Storage and Security

User information may be stored in the application's database to support account management and application functionality. We take reasonable steps to protect stored information against unauthorized access or misuse.

However, no digital system or method of data transmission can guarantee complete security.

5. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide application functionality, such as AI response generation through the Gemini API.

6. User Responsibilities

  • Keep account credentials confidential.
  • Provide only information necessary to use the application.
  • Avoid submitting sensitive personal or financial credentials.
  • Log out after using the application on shared devices.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to support application functionality and maintain user records. The actual retention period depends on the application's database and administrative practices.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users should obtain appropriate parental or guardian guidance where required.

9. External Links

The application may contain links to external websites or third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or requirements change. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Information

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the project development team through the appropriate project communication channel.

Your Privacy Matters: Please use the application responsibly and avoid sharing passwords, banking credentials, or other highly sensitive personal information with the AI Assistant.
